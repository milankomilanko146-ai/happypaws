The story behind this

I Was Embarrassed to Walk My Own Dog.

My name is Sarah McKinnon — certified dog trainer (CPDT-KA), 11 years of experience. And for the first two years of owning my golden retriever Biscuit, I was doing it completely wrong.

I'd spent $1,200 on three trainers. Watched every YouTube video. Bought the clicker, the prong collar, the spray. Nothing lasted longer than a week. The moment we got home from class, Biscuit forgot everything.

"The problem wasn't my dog. The problem was that nobody ever taught me how to be consistent in a real home — not in a kennel, not in a class."

So I rebuilt my approach from scratch for home environments. I tested it with 312 Canadian families — condos, apartments, houses, big dogs, tiny dogs, puppies and 9-year-olds. 94% saw measurable change within 7 days. That system is The Good Dog Method.

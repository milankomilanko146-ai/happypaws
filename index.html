Based on your answers, your skin is experiencing a pattern seen in the majority of women who visit dermatology clinics — a problem caused not by age or genetics, but by the wrong products.

Written by Dr. Marta Novak, MD Board-certified dermatologist · Skin barrier & inflammation specialist

Every week in my clinic, I see the same pattern. A woman comes in frustrated. She has tried cleanser after cleanser, moisturiser after moisturiser — often expensive, always "dermatologist tested." Her skin keeps reacting: redness that won't settle, breakouts with no clear cause, tightness that gets worse despite applying more cream.

The cause is almost always the same thing: she has been unknowingly damaging her skin's protective barrier. And the products she is using to fix the problem are, in many cases, the very products making it worse.

The skin barrier is a thin protective layer — the outermost surface — that keeps moisture in and irritants out. When it is intact, skin looks calm, even, and hydrated. When it is disrupted, skin becomes reactive, inflamed, and visibly damaged over time. Crucially, barrier disruption does not always feel dramatic. It often starts with mild sensitivity, then progresses slowly — until one day the skin simply stops responding to anything.

"The barrier can be repaired. In most cases, it takes between 3 and 6 weeks of using the right formulations consistently. I have seen this happen hundreds of times in clinical practice."

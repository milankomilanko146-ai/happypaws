Frequently Asked Questions
Does it work without a subscription? +

Yes — SmartView Pro works completely without any monthly fee. Live view, two-way audio and instant motion alerts are all free. Optional cloud storage plans are available if you want 30-day video history, but they're never required.

Is it battery-powered or wired? +

Both. SmartView Pro supports wired installation (connects to your existing doorbell wiring) and wireless battery mode. The built-in rechargeable battery lasts 3–6 months on a single charge depending on usage.

What smart home systems does it work with? +

SmartView Pro is compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Home and Apple HomeKit. You can view live footage on Echo Show, Google Nest Hub or any Apple HomeKit-enabled display.

How good is the night vision? +

Excellent. Our advanced infrared night vision provides clear, detailed footage up to 10 metres in complete darkness. The camera automatically switches between colour and night vision mode depending on light conditions.

What if it doesn't work for me? +

You're covered by our 30-day no-questions-asked return policy. If you're not completely satisfied, simply use our online returns portal to send it back for a full refund. We also offer a 2-year manufacturer warranty on all units.

How does the bundle discount work? +

Buying 2 units saves you 10% and buying 3 saves you 16%. The discount is applied automatically when you select your bundle above — no coupon codes needed. Many customers cover their front door and back entrance together.

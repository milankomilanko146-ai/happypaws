Frequently Asked Questions

How does cervical traction work? ▾
Cervical traction gently stretches the neck to decompress the cervical spine. This creates space between vertebrae, relieving pressure on pinched nerves and herniated discs. The SpineRevive Pro™ uses pneumatic air cells that you control with a hand pump, allowing precise, comfortable traction force between 20-60 lbs.
How often should I use it? ▸
We recommend starting with 10-minute sessions, 1-2 times daily. Most users work up to 15-20 minute sessions over the first week. The built-in auto-shutoff ensures safe use. Consistent daily use yields the best results, with most customers reporting significant relief within 2-4 weeks.
Is it safe to use at home without supervision? ▸
Yes. The SpineRevive Pro™ is designed for safe home use. It features adjustable pressure control, a built-in pressure gauge, and a 15-minute auto-shutoff. However, if you have a serious spinal condition, fractures, or spinal instability, please consult your doctor before use.

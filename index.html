The Problem With How We Treat Sore Muscles

When your muscles hurt, the go-to solutions are pretty predictable:

The Usual Suspects
  • Ibuprofen or pain killers — works short-term, but masks the pain without treating what's underneath. And daily use isn't great for your stomach or kidneys.
  • Ice packs or heat pads — decent for acute injuries, but barely touch deep muscle tension.
  • Stretching — good habit, but if the underlying circulation is poor, the soreness keeps coming back.
  • Massage therapy — genuinely helps, but at $80–$150 per session, it's not something most people can do regularly.

None of these get to the actual root of the issue.

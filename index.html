FAQ

How does the Red Light Face Mask work? +
Red light therapy uses specific wavelengths (630nm red and 850nm near-infrared) to penetrate the skin at different depths. This stimulates mitochondria to produce more ATP energy, boosting collagen, accelerating cell repair, and reducing inflammation — resulting in smoother, firmer skin.
Is it safe for all skin types? +
Yes. Clinically tested and dermatologist-approved for all skin types including sensitive skin. The LED technology is non-invasive, non-thermal, and completely safe for home use. Consult your doctor if you have a medical condition or are photosensitive.
How long until I see results? +
Most customers notice improved skin texture and radiance within 2–4 weeks (3–5 sessions per week). Significant results — reduced fine lines, improved skin tone — are typically visible after 6–8 weeks of consistent use.
How long does each session last? +
Each session should last 10–20 minutes. Start with 10 minutes and gradually increase to 20. The mask automatically turns off after 20 minutes for safety.
